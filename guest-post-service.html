Guest Post Service

Publish useful articles for the right audience

Earn attention from readers who already care about your industry. Develop original guest articles with useful examples, a strong editorial fit, and honest performance reporting.

Discuss Your Project →All Services →

What the service covers

Publisher and audience research

Evaluate topical fit, editorial standards, audience relevance, and existing articles. A third-party authority score alone tells you little about whether readers will care.

Original article and pitch

Develop an angle that fits the publisher’s coverage. Useful formats include field-tested checklists, data with a clear source, and explanations that spare readers a common mistake.

Placement and measurement

Record the publication URL, disclosure status, link attributes, and destination. Measure referral visits or relevant inquiries when attribution is available.

How the work starts

  1. Agree on the audience, publication criteria, and useful topic ideas.
  2. Review the draft for accuracy and submit it for the publisher’s independent editorial review.
  3. Verify the final placement and track opportunities for relevant referral traffic.

What to check before getting started

Pitch an article that answers a question the publisher’s readers already ask. For example, a roofing contractor could explain which attic inspection findings warrant a professional assessment, without turning the article into an advertisement.

If you already have a useful article idea, see how Grid Rankers handles guest post outreach and placement. See Google’s link spam policy for the platform’s current rules.

Frequently asked questions

Are guest posts a rankings guarantee?

No. Guest posting can introduce you to readers, but search engines may ignore links, and ranking changes cannot be reliably attributed to a placement alone.

How should paid placement links be marked?

Google recommends rel="sponsored" for advertisements or paid placements; rel="nofollow" is also acceptable. Commercial relationships should be disclosed where required.

Can I choose the anchor text?

You can suggest wording, but the link should help the reader and fit the sentence. Let the publisher retain editorial control.
