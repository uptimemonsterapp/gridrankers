Press Release Distribution

Share a newsworthy announcement clearly

Turn openings, expansions, partnerships, and verified milestones into announcements that journalists and industry readers can quickly understand.

Discuss Your Project →All Services →

What the service covers

News angle and fact-checking

Identify what changed, when, where, and why it matters to the intended audience. Confirm names, dates, claims, and quotes before anything is distributed.

Release development and distribution

Prepare a headline, dateline, useful opening paragraph, background, and media contact. Select distribution options based on geography and topic rather than raw outlet counts.

Reporting and follow-up

Separate syndication pickups from independently reported stories. Track relevant referral visits and inquiries if measurement is set up before distribution.

How the work starts

  1. Gather the announcement details, approved quotes, and any images you have rights to use.
  2. Review the facts and timing with stakeholders and select the distribution scope.
  3. Document pickups and review whether targeted follow-up pitches are justified.

What to check before getting started

Use the first paragraph to answer the five Ws. If the announcement is a location opening, include the address, opening date, what customers can do there, and how they can obtain details. Put promotional language below the facts, if it belongs at all.

If you have an approved announcement and a release date, compare Grid Rankers’ press release distribution options. See Google’s link spam policy for the platform’s current rules.

Frequently asked questions

Does distribution guarantee media coverage?

No. Syndication can reproduce the release, but editors independently decide whether to report a story.

Do syndicated links guarantee SEO gains?

No. Treat the release as communication, not as a source of ranking credit. Paid links should be qualified appropriately.

What is worth announcing?

A new location with a firm opening date, a partnership that changes customer access, or data with a clear methodology gives readers a concrete reason to care.
