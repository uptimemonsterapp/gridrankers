GBP Reinstatement

Prepare a stronger appeal for a suspended profile

A suspension can interrupt calls and bookings. We help you review profile eligibility, correct conflicting information, and prepare a documented Google Business Profile appeal.

Discuss Your Project →All Services →

What the service covers

Eligibility and policy review

Review the notification, recent edits, ownership, address model, duplicate listings, and business name. A suspension notice may not spell out the underlying issue.

Evidence and corrections

Organize documents such as a business license, registration, or utility bill when applicable. Match the details to the profile and share evidence through the appropriate Google channel.

Appeal readiness

Summarize the issue, what changed, and why the business qualifies. Keep a record of the submitted evidence and follow Google’s available review options if the appeal is denied.

How the work starts

  1. Collect the notice and profile history without sending passwords or sensitive documents by ordinary email.
  2. Correct confirmed issues and prepare supporting evidence before starting the appeal.
  3. Track the appeal status, then check the visible listing details if the profile returns.

What to check before getting started

Before opening the appeal, save the notice and collect supporting documents that match the business name and operating model. Confirm whether the restriction applies to a single listing or the account managing it; the next step can differ.

If you need help reviewing the notice and preparing evidence, explore Grid Rankers’ Google Business Profile reinstatement service. See Google’s Business Profile appeals guidance for the platform’s current rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee reinstatement?

No. Google decides whether to reinstate the profile and how long the review takes.

Should I create a replacement profile?

Do not create a replacement for the same business while an appeal is under review. It can introduce duplicates and make the situation harder to resolve.

Can a virtual address qualify?

A rented mailing address or virtual office is not eligible on its own. If you serve customers at their locations and otherwise qualify, follow the rules for a service-area business.
